How to test your SIM

A website cannot directly read the SIM or the router’s cellular radio. To test mobile/SIM speed, turn off Wi‑Fi on your phone, make sure mobile data is on, then tap Run speed test. To test the SIM inside your 5G router, connect to the router’s Wi‑Fi—the results measure the full route: router cellular link plus Wi‑Fi.

Important: “3G”, “4G”, or “5G” shown by browsers is only an estimate of connection quality. This app now labels the connection as Wi‑Fi when your device reports Wi‑Fi, instead of incorrectly treating the estimate as your router’s cellular generation.
